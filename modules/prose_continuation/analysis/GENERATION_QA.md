model writes meta-text instead of continuation-like text.

## Target-Prefix Hits

The scan also checked whether the first 40 or 120 chars of the scored target
appeared inside extracted `Z`. These hits are not automatically bad because
the predictor is supposed to forecast `Y`, and technical text often repeats
local phrases.

Counts:

```text
target_first_120_chars_in_z:
  gpt55_none: 2
  gpt55_low:  2
  all other lanes: 0

target_first_40_chars_in_z:
  gpt55_none: 15
  gpt55_low: 15
  gpt55_medium: 12
  gpt55_high: 12
  nano_low: 7
  nano_medium: 8
  nano_high: 8
```

The four 120-char hits were hand-read. They look like ordinary near-forecast /
repeated-structure cases, not scoring-prompt leakage:

- `2604.21800`, cut `133`, `gpt55_none` and `gpt55_low`: the model predicts
  the start of a statement about \(G\)-symmetric detecting projectors and
  then diverges in coefficient notation/details.
- `2604.20750`, cut `204`, `gpt55_none` and `gpt55_low`: the model predicts
  the continuation of a TikZ diagram node block and then diverges into
  additional diagram arrows, while the true target transitions to a section
  outline.

These are exactly the sort of partial local hits the reward is meant to detect.
No evidence from this audit suggests wrapper text, prompt construction error,
or accidental inclusion of `Y` in the prompt.
